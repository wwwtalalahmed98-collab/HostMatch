What this cannot tell you

  • No titre, yield or probability of success. The simulation step that would predict those needs a proteome-constrained metabolic model, which runs in MATLAB and is not wired in.
  • The budget bar is a sweep across an assumption. Nobody has measured what any of these modules costs the cell, so the 40% ceiling cannot yet be checked against a real answer.
  • The signal peptide and transmembrane calls are hydropathy heuristics. Real answers need SignalP and DeepTMHMM. They are marked in the readout.
  • O-glycosylation and other modifications come back unknown, not zero. They cannot be read off a sequence here.
  • This chassis does not exist. It is a base organism plus five proposed modules, each carrying published precedent rather than a validated construct.
  • No validation against measured outcomes. A published result reports that architecturally similar antibody fragments behave differently under identical engineering, so sequence alone has a ceiling no model removes.
